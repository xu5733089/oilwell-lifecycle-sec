Definitions. For purposes of this Item 1208, the following terms shall be defined as indicated:

## [Item 1208(c)(1)] Definitions · 油气资产、井与面积
<!-- meta: {"cfr": "17 CFR 229.1208(c)(1)", "parent": "Item 1208(c)", "kind": "paragraph", "level": 2, "regulation": "Regulation S-K", "section_head": "§ 229.1208 (Item 1208) Oil and gas properties, wells, operations, and acreage.", "zh_title": "油气资产、井与面积", "source": "eCFR 2025-01-01"} -->
A gross well or acre is a well or acre in which the registrant owns a working interest. The number of gross wells is the total number of wells in which the registrant owns a working interest. Count one or more completions in the same bore hole as one well. In a footnote, disclose the number of wells with multiple completions. If one of the multiple completions in a well is an oil completion, classify the well as an oil well.

## [Item 1208(c)(2)] Definitions · 油气资产、井与面积
<!-- meta: {"cfr": "17 CFR 229.1208(c)(2)", "parent": "Item 1208(c)", "kind": "paragraph", "level": 2, "regulation": "Regulation S-K", "section_head": "§ 229.1208 (Item 1208) Oil and gas properties, wells, operations, and acreage.", "zh_title": "油气资产、井与面积", "source": "eCFR 2025-01-01"} -->
A net well or acre is deemed to exist when the sum of fractional ownership working interests in gross wells or acres equals one. The number of net wells or acres is the sum of the fractional working interests owned in gross wells or acres expressed as whole numbers and fractions of whole numbers.

## [Item 1208(c)(3)] Definitions · 油气资产、井与面积
<!-- meta: {"cfr": "17 CFR 229.1208(c)(3)", "parent": "Item 1208(c)", "kind": "paragraph", "level": 2, "regulation": "Regulation S-K", "section_head": "§ 229.1208 (Item 1208) Oil and gas properties, wells, operations, and acreage.", "zh_title": "油气资产、井与面积", "source": "eCFR 2025-01-01"} -->
Productive wells include producing wells and wells mechanically capable of production.

## [Item 1208(c)(4)] Definitions · 油气资产、井与面积
<!-- meta: {"cfr": "17 CFR 229.1208(c)(4)", "parent": "Item 1208(c)", "kind": "paragraph", "level": 2, "regulation": "Regulation S-K", "section_head": "§ 229.1208 (Item 1208) Oil and gas properties, wells, operations, and acreage.", "zh_title": "油气资产、井与面积", "source": "eCFR 2025-01-01"} -->
Undeveloped acreage encompasses those leased acres on which wells have not been drilled or completed to a point that would permit the production of economic quantities of oil or gas regardless of whether such acreage contains proved reserves. Do not confuse undeveloped acreage with undrilled acreage held by production under the terms of the lease.
